SL · FIRST ASSESSMENT 2027

Know your assessment

Keep the SL assessment structure separate from older course materials and HL-only requirements.

EXTERNAL · 35%

Paper 1

1 hour 30 minutes · 35 marks

Three sections testing content knowledge, application to contexts and conceptual thinking.

  • Section A: two compulsory short-answer questions from two of the three content areas.
  • Section B: two compulsory questions applying content to unseen situations.
  • Section C: one concept-based extended response from a choice of two.
EXTERNAL · 35%

Paper 2

1 hour 30 minutes · 35 marks

Focuses on the class practicals and critical evaluation of research.

  • Section A: four compulsory questions focused on class practicals.
  • Section B: evaluate an unseen research study with reference to two or more concepts.
INTERNAL · 30%

Research proposal

24 marks · approximately 20 hours

Design a proposal to investigate a population of interest using one of the four class-practical methods.

  • Experiment
  • Interview
  • Observation
  • Survey / questionnaire

The task is to design and justify a proposal; it is not a requirement to carry out the study.

SL note: Paper 3 is an HL paper, not part of SL assessment. Your uploaded Data Interpretation chapter is retained here as a useful research-method reference; the new guide identifies data analysis and interpretation as directly assessed at HL.
