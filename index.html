RESEARCH METHODS

THINK LIKE A PSYCHOLOGIST

The six key concepts

Use these concepts to analyse and evaluate psychological research.

01

Bias

Systematic influences that may distort research or interpretation.

02

Causality

Whether one variable can be said to cause a change in another.

03

Change

How psychological characteristics and behaviour develop over time.

04

Measurement

How psychological variables are defined and measured.

05

Perspective

How different viewpoints explain behaviour and mental processes.

06

Responsibility

Ethical obligations and the impact of psychological knowledge.

EXAM PREPARATION

Know your assessment

IB Psychology SL first assessment 2027.

EXTERNAL ASSESSMENT

Paper 1

1 hour 30 minutes

Short-answer questions, application to an unseen situation, and a concept-based extended response.

  • Section A: content knowledge
  • Section B: context application
  • Section C: concept-based response
EXTERNAL ASSESSMENT

Paper 2

1 hour 30 minutes

Questions about your class practicals and evaluation of an unseen research study.

  • Section A: class practicals
  • Section B: unseen research study
  • Apply at least two relevant concepts
INTERNAL ASSESSMENT

Research proposal

30% of final grade

Design a research proposal to investigate a population of interest using one of the taught research methods.

  • 24 marks
  • Approximately 20 hours
  • One of four research methods

SL students do not sit Paper 3. Always check the latest subject guide and your teacher's instructions.
